## Our Mission

ToolVX was built with a single, unwavering principle: **your files should never leave your device.**

In an era where most online PDF and image tools require you to upload sensitive documents to unknown servers — exposing contracts, financial records, medical reports, and personal identification to potential data breaches — ToolVX offers a fundamentally different approach. We provide a comprehensive, browser-based processing workstation where **every operation runs 100% client-side**, directly in your web browser. No uploads. No servers. No data collection. Zero risk of data leakage.

## What We Offer

ToolVX brings together **sixteen (16) professional-grade tools** in one unified, privacy-first workspace:

### PDF & Image Redaction
Permanently remove sensitive text, images, and metadata from PDFs and images using solid-fill, mosaic, and custom redaction methods. Ideal for freelancers, legal professionals, and compliance teams who need to share documents without exposing confidential information.

### PDF Compression
Reduce PDF file sizes without sacrificing readability — perfect for email attachments and document submissions with size limits.

### PDF Merge & Split
Combine multiple PDFs into a single document or extract specific pages from a large file. No need to install heavy desktop software.

### PDF Rotate & Page Deletion
Reorient scanned pages and remove unwanted pages with a few clicks.

### PDF Metadata Editing
View and edit PDF document properties — title, author, subject, keywords — to keep your files organized and professional.

### PDF Reader
A fast, lightweight in-browser PDF reader with zoom, navigation, and full-screen support. No plugins required.

### PDF Page Numbering
Add customizable page numbers to your documents for reports, manuscripts, and official filings.

### PDF Cropping
Trim margins and resize PDF pages to exact dimensions.

### PDF Watermark
Add text or image watermarks to protect your documents from unauthorized use.

### PDF ↔ JPG Conversion
Convert PDF pages to high-quality JPG images, or combine JPG images into a single PDF document.

### PDF Signing
Apply digital signatures to your PDF documents directly in the browser.

### PDF Unlock & Protect
Remove password protection from PDFs or add strong encryption to secure your sensitive files.

## Our Technology

All ToolVX tools are built with modern web technologies — **JavaScript, WebAssembly, and the HTML5 Canvas API** — running entirely in your browser's local runtime. We leverage libraries such as [PDF.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/) and [pdf-lib](https://pdf-lib.js.org/) to deliver desktop-class PDF processing without any server-side dependency.

This architecture means:

- **Instant processing** — no waiting for file uploads or server queues.
- **Complete privacy** — your files never traverse the network.
- **No account required** — just open the tool and start working.
- **Cross-platform** — works on Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile browsers.
