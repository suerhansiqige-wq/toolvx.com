# Contact Us

We welcome your feedback, partnership proposals, and technical suggestions. At ToolVX, we believe that open communication helps us build better products and serve our global community of users more effectively.

## How to Reach Us

For all inquiries — including business cooperation, advertising partnerships, technical feedback, and bug reports — please reach out to us directly via email:

## [admin@toolvx.com](mailto:admin@toolvx.com)

We aim to respond to all legitimate inquiries within **2–5 business days**.

## What You Can Contact Us About

- **Business Cooperation & Partnerships** — If you are interested in integrating ToolVX technology into your workflow, exploring co-marketing opportunities, or discussing enterprise licensing, we would love to hear from you.
- **Technical Feedback & Feature Requests** — Your input drives our product roadmap. Whether you have encountered a bug, have a suggestion for improving an existing tool, or want to propose a brand-new feature, our engineering team reviews every piece of feedback.
- **Advertising & Sponsorship** — For advertising inquiries, sponsored content proposals, or media partnership opportunities, please include your organization name, proposal summary, and expected timeline.
- **Privacy & Data Security Concerns** — If you have questions about how ToolVX handles your data, our compliance with GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy regulations, or you wish to exercise your data subject rights, please reach out and reference our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
- **Press & Media Inquiries** — Journalists and content creators seeking interviews, product demos, or press materials should contact us with their publication name and deadline.

## What to Include in Your Message

To help us respond to your inquiry as quickly as possible, please include the following information:

1. **Your name and organization** (if applicable)
2. **A clear subject line** summarizing the topic
3. **Relevant details** — such as the specific tool you used, your browser and operating system, and steps to reproduce any issues
4. **Supporting files** — screenshots, sample documents, or error messages that help us understand your situation

## Our Commitment

ToolVX is built by a dedicated team committed to privacy-first document processing. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — your files are never uploaded to our servers. If you have concerns about data handling or need clarification on our security practices, we are transparent and happy to explain.

For urgent security disclosures, please mark your email subject line with **[SECURITY]** and include a detailed description of the vulnerability.
