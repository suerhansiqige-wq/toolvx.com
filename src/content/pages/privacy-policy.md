## 1. Introduction

Welcome to ToolVX ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website at [https://toolvx.com](https://toolvx.com) (the "Site").

ToolVX is a **privacy-first, browser-based PDF and image processing platform**. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## 2. Core Privacy Principle: 100% Client-Side Processing

**This is the most important part of our Privacy Policy.**

All sixteen (16) tools provided by ToolVX — including but not limited to PDF & Image Redaction, PDF Compression, PDF Merge, PDF Split, PDF Rotate, PDF Page Deletion, PDF Metadata Editing, PDF Reader, PDF Page Numbering, PDF Cropping, PDF Watermark, PDF-to-JPG Conversion, JPG-to-PDF Conversion, PDF Signing, PDF Unlock, and PDF Protect — operate exclusively through **100% client-side web-browser processing**.

This means:

- **No backend server storage.** We do not operate any server that receives, stores, processes, or retains your PDF documents, images, or any file data.
- **No file uploads.** Your files are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party server for processing.
- **No data interception.** We do not intercept, inspect, scan, or analyze the content of any file you process using our tools.
- **No pixel-level data collection.** We do not capture, record, or transmit any pixel data, text content, metadata, or document structure from your files.
- **No data retention.** Because your files never leave your device, there is nothing for us to retain, log, or delete.

All processing occurs locally within your web browser's runtime environment using JavaScript, WebAssembly, and the HTML5 Canvas API. Your files remain on your device at all times.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our Site, we (and our third-party service providers) may automatically collect certain non-personal technical information, including:

- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referring URL
- Pages visited and time spent on pages
- IP address (anonymized where possible)
- General geographic location (country/region level, derived from IP)

This information is collected through standard web server logs and analytics cookies. It does **not** include any information about the files you process using our tools.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our Site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.org](https://www.aboutads.org/choices/).

## 5. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right to access** — You may request information about the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to rectification** — You may request correction of inaccurate personal data.
- **Right to erasure** — You may request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Right to restrict processing** — You may request that we limit how we use your personal data.
- **Right to data portability** — You may request a copy of your personal data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to object** — You may object to our processing of your personal data.
- **Right to withdraw consent** — Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **admin@toolvx.com**.

Since our tools process all data client-side and we do not store your files, there is no file data for us to delete or export upon request.

## 6. CCPA Compliance (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to know** — You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
- **Right to delete** — You may request deletion of your personal information.
- **Right to opt-out** — You may opt out of the "sale" of your personal information. (We do not sell personal information.)
- **Right to non-discrimination** — You will not be discriminated against for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise your CCPA rights, please contact us at **admin@toolvx.com**.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.
