### The Hidden Trap of Cloud-Based Redaction Tools

When you look for a tool to blur an ID card, cover a password, or redact sensitive pixels in a screenshot, most online tools require you to upload your image to their cloud servers. The moment your image leaves your local machine, you lose control over your data. If those servers are breached, or if the provider stores logs, your sensitive data becomes compromised.
