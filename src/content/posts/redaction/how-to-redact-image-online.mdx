## Why You Need a Secure Solution to Redact Image Online

Most free online image editors are dangerous. They ask you to upload your file. They store it on their server. They keep a copy forever.

Your sensitive data is now on someone else's machine. That is a massive risk.

### The Problem with Server-Side Tools

Here is what happens with typical online editors:

- You upload your image to their server.
- Their server processes the file.
- A copy remains in their database.
- You have no control over deletion.
- Their privacy policy may allow data sharing.

This is the opposite of privacy. Your screenshot, your ID photo, your document — all stored on a third-party server.

### The Solution: Client-Side Processing

A secure tool to **redact image online** works differently. It runs entirely in your browser. The file never leaves your device.

Here is how client-side processing works:

1. Your browser loads the tool's JavaScript code.
2. You drop your image into the browser window.
3. The tool processes the image using HTML5 Canvas.
4. All pixel manipulation happens locally on your machine.
5. You download the redacted file. No upload. No server. No logs.

This is the gold standard for online privacy. Your data stays on your device at all times.

### Step 3: Choose Your Mask Style

This is where the technical difference matters. You have two main options:

**Solid Color Fill (红色块遮蔽)**

This method overwrites every pixel in the selected area with a single solid color. Usually red or black. The original pixel data is completely destroyed. There is zero chance of recovery. This is the most secure option.

**Mosaic (马赛克遮蔽)**

This method divides the selected area into small blocks. Each block's pixels are averaged into one color. The result is a pixelated effect. Larger blocks mean less detail. Smaller blocks retain more visual context. Mosaic is secure but slightly less absolute than solid fill.

| Feature | Solid Color Fill | Mosaic |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| Pixel destruction | Complete | Complete |
| Recovery risk | Zero | Zero |
| Visual result | Clean block | Pixelated grid |
| Best for | IDs, faces, text | Backgrounds, large areas |

**Fuzzy Radius (模糊遮蔽)**

This applies a soft blur to the edges of the redaction area. It makes the boundary look cleaner. The core pixels are still destroyed.

### Step 4: Download the Clean File

Click the download button. Save your redacted image. The exported file contains only the masked pixels. The original data is gone permanently.

## FAQ: Common Questions About Online Image Redaction

### Is it safe to redact images online?

It depends on the tool. Server-side editors that require uploads are not safe. Your file is stored on their server. Browser-based, client-side tools are safe. Your file never leaves your device. Always verify the tool's processing model before use.

### Can AI reverse an online photo redaction?

If the redaction used solid color fill or mosaic at the pixel level, no. The original data is permanently destroyed. AI cannot recover what no longer exists. However, if the tool only added a visual overlay layer, AI can easily remove it. Always use pixel-level redaction.

### What is the difference between blurring and redacting an image?

Blurring softens pixel values using a filter. Advanced algorithms can sometimes reverse blur effects. True redaction replaces pixels entirely with solid colors or mosaic patterns. Redaction is permanent. Blurring is not. For sensitive data, always choose redaction over blur.
