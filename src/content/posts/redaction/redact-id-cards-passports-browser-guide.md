### High-Stakes Privacy: Identity Documents

Submitting passport scans or driver's licenses for online verification is common, but leaving your signature, document number, or tracking barcodes exposed can lead to severe financial fraud.

### The Ultimate Sanitize Checklist

Before transmitting document scans, use **ToolVX** client-side components to black out:

- National identity registration numbers.
- Home addresses and personal contact details.
- Machine-Readable Zone (MRZ) barcodes at the bottom of passports.
